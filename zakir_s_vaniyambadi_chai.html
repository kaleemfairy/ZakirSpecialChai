Our Traditional Heritage

The Story Behind Vaniyambadi Dum Brewing

Unlike conventional street tea where milk and water boil together continuously until tannin becomes bitter and harsh, Vaniyambadi Dum Chai extracts flavor through sealed steam pressure.

The decoction pot remains tightly covered over embers for 45 minutes, gently releasing volatile spice aromatics and color. When combined with slow-simmered farm buffalo milk and aerated with a high-arching meter pour, it creates our velvety consistency without excess sugar.

100% Pure Buffalo Milk Procured fresh every morning from local farms.
Zero Artificial Additives No tea essences, artificial syrups, or stabilizers.

Why Unglazed Kulhars Enhance Chai

When piping hot chai touches raw, unglazed terracotta pottery, microscopic clay pores absorb excess moisture and release a subtle mineral sweetness known throughout India as sondhi khushboo.
